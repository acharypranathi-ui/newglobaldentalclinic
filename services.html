Multidisciplinary Care

Our 10 Key Specializations

Expertise covering every aspect of oral health, surgery, and aesthetic smile design.

1. Dental Implantology

Advanced replacement solutions for missing teeth using single implants, implant-supported bridges, and full-mouth rehabilitations.

2. Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery

Surgical treatments of dental defects, wisdom teeth removal, cyst extraction, and major reconstructive procedures.

3. Craniofacial Surgery

Highly specialized treatment focusing on jaw joint correction, jaw reconstruction, orthognathic surgery, and facial trauma management.

4. Cosmetic Dentistry

Smile enhancements via veneers, laminates, high-end teeth whitening, and complete smile makeover options.

5. General Dentistry

Comprehensive oral diagnostic services, teeth fillings, regular dental checks, and health maintenance.

6. Restorative Dentistry

Restoring tooth function using durable zirconium crowns, porcelain bridges, fillings, and structural restorations.

7. Root Canal Treatment

Endodontic procedure to preserve infected tooth structure, remove pain, and prevent bone damage.

8. Preventive Dental Care

Proactive care programs: professional scaling/cleaning, sealants, fluoride, and early decay tracking.

9. Full-Mouth Rehabilitation

Comprehensive reconstruction planning for cases with multiple decayed, broken, or heavily missing teeth.

10. Smile Designing

Personalized aesthetic design protocols combining whitening, aligners, braces, veneers, and contours.
