Doctor-Approved Daily At-Home Protocol

Daily habits recommended by clinical experts for maintaining peak dental health between clinic visits:

The 2x2 Method

Brush twice a day for two full minutes using soft-bristled oscillating electric brushes and fluoridated or hydroxyapatite toothpaste.

Interdental Cleaning Daily

Use dental floss or water flossers before bedtime to clear biofilm where bristles cannot reach.

Tongue Cleansing

Gently clean the tongue surface to eliminate volatile sulfur compounds and maintain balanced breath.

Protect Against Bruxism

If you awaken with jaw stiffness or morning headaches, request a custom night guard to protect enamel.
